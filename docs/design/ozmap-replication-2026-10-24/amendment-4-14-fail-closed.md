# A4.14: fail closed execution and convergent review (2026-10-25)

Reason. Three council rounds on the build gave 34, 25 and 25 blockers. Most open items are crash recovery edge cases (in flight recovery, PID journals, superseded batches, resumed cells, reseed races) introduced by the driver's resumable cell design, not by Amendment 4. Each fix opens new edges and fresh reviewers find them. The owner approved replacing recovery with fail closed execution and a convergent review rule. This revision is prospective and changes no evaluation cap, gate, prompt or workload.

A4.14.1. Fail closed execution. The harness performs no in flight recovery and never resumes or reruns a cell within a run. If a cell meets a driver or runner crash, an unsettled or unknown call or process state, an unexpected authentication state, an isolation or leakage signal, or an accounting anomaly, the harness stops that cell and preserves every record. The scorer then applies A4.3 and A4.11 to the preserved records of every stopped cell. A leakage or isolation signal, a demonstrated accounting failure, or uncertainty about whether a tool executed, including an unsettled or unknown call state, makes the cell invalid under A4.3 and A4.11, not merely interrupted. Only cells with no such finding are marked interrupted. Calls already dispatched count against caps. Interruption does not establish that dispatched work has stopped. Before admitting another cell, the harness must establish that outstanding work from the interrupted cell cannot affect that cell’s isolation, authentication, records or accounting. If this cannot be established, execution stops for the run and no further cells are admitted. Outstanding calls retain their accounting obligations under the existing rules. Establishing this containment does not permit resuming or rerunning the interrupted cell. An interrupted cell's comparisons are inconclusive under A4.11. A cell whose prerequisite cell is interrupted (for example warm C after an interrupted cold bootstrap) is interrupted without running. The A4.2 stop rules for Codex evaluation are unchanged and remain stricter where they apply.

A4.14.2. Blocker rubric. A review item is a BLOCKER only if it can let an invalid, leaked, misaccounted or wrong result reach the result report without being flagged, or can harm the owner's own Codex login or accounts, and it comes with a reproducing test or an exact command transcript, or a precise code path for a review that cannot run code. An issue whose worst outcome is a stopped, interrupted or inconclusive cell is a CHANGE, because A4.14.1 already fails closed on it. Any path that labels a detected or detectable validity failure as interrupted or inconclusive, or that lets a stopped cell's records go unscored, is a BLOCKER.

A4.14.3. Convergent review. After the fail closed simplification, at most two closure rounds follow. In a closure round each reviewer checks every open blocker from earlier rounds and states closed, moot under A4.14.1, or open with evidence, and may raise a new BLOCKER only under A4.14.2. If blockers remain after the second closure round, the workflow stops for the owner.

A4.14.4. Removal and tests. The harness may delete recovery and resume machinery. Acceptance and red team tests that encode recovery or resume behavior are replaced by tests asserting the A4.14.1 behavior: the cell is marked interrupted, records are preserved, nothing is rerun, dependent cells are interrupted. Each replaced test is listed with its replacement in the build notes. Tests also verify that unresolved outstanding work prevents further cell admission unless the containment required by A4.14.1 is established. Removal never touches A4.2 authentication propagation, the hash comparison, the exp gate or the no-restore rule.

A4.14.5. Unchanged. Isolation, authentication safety, leakage detection, tool event handling, usage and cache accounting, the safety kill, admission, scoring, gates and provenance stay as signed. Fail closed never turns a detected validity failure into a merely interrupted cell; A4.3 and A4.11 still decide validity.
